Dot Grid Cards

Rs. 2,900

455 reviews

Description

A flexible grid of dots for sketching, planning, or writing without lines getting in the way. Each card is printed on thick, smooth paper that takes ink without bleeding through to the other side.

The dots are light enough to fade into the background when you write, and clear enough to keep your lines straight when you need them. Use them for quick diagrams, weekly plans, meeting notes or rough layouts.

Every pack comes with fifty cards, and the corners are rounded so they don't snag or fray in a bag. They fit standard card holders, so you can stand a day's plan upright on your desk where you can see it.

Use a fine pen for neat handwriting, a pencil for sketches you may want to change, or a marker for bold headings. The paper handles all of them, and the cards stay flat instead of curling.

The cards are designed to be used one idea at a time. Write a single task, a single sketch or a single thought on each card, then rearrange them as your day changes. When a card is done, move it to the back of the stack or file it away.

Refill packs are sold separately, so you can keep the same holder for years and only replace the cards you use up. Because the sizes are standard, you can mix and match formats in one stack.

Care is simple: keep the cards dry, and store them flat in a drawer or a holder. If a card gets bent, press it under a book overnight and it will flatten out again.
